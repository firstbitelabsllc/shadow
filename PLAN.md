# Vidux — Evidence-First Orchestration Cockpit

## Purpose
Make Vidux the opinionated, batteries-included operating cockpit for people running Claude, Codex, and other agents across multiple projects. It must turn scattered work into one inspectable next move, preserve plan/proof/resume truth across tools, and earn its overhead with quantitative outcome and cost evidence instead of orchestration theater.

The 2026-07-07 kernel cut remains valid: the tested planner/executor handoff lost, so it stays frozen. That result killed a weak architecture, not the product mission. Vidux may recommend and coordinate bounded workers through the host runtime, but it must never hide routing cost, claim an unmeasured win, or make its own handoff protocol the product.
