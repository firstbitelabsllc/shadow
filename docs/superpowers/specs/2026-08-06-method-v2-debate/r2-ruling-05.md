# ROUND 2 RULING — Cluster: checkpoint row tokens

**Central question answered first:** No — markdown-with-git does not honestly do concurrency. Three independent proofs: (1) the identical-edit blind spot — two seats flipping the same row `[pending]`→`[completed]` make identical edits that git's ort merge resolves silently, so the "merge conflict IS the collision detector" law cannot see the exact double-flip it exists for, and its Progress-anchor sensitivity *decays under load* (r1-seat-09.md, Arg A); (2) the machinery is unpracticed in its own house — `grep -c CLAIM` = 0 in the flagship plan while PROOF/DONE exist, and one line batch-DONEs four rows against one-claim-per-loop (r1-seat-09.md, Arg B); (3) no code writes or reads any of these verbs — the 1,190-line drive engine has zero hits for CLAIM/PROOF/DONE (r1-seat-04.md, Arg A). The chair's defer-trigger ("until the failure actually occurs") is broken by construction: silent merges mean the failure is unobservable, so "we have never had one collision" is unfalsifiable, not evidence. The honest core is **one seat per plan at a time + proof lines**. My rulings implement exactly that.

---

**1. `~hash4` row IDs — KEEP-CORE (as row address; mint recipe FOLDED).**
Rent paid: the hash is not concurrency machinery — it is the row's *address*, consumed by every surviving mechanism: `needs:` targets and the launch-lockdown DAG (r1-seat-05.md, Arg B), PROOF Progress lines, steering option lines, archive tombstones whose `needs:`/`from:` lineage survives relocation — "the archive hook is already built and unused" (r1-seat-03.md, proposal), and the only mechanical-acceptance proposal on the table addresses rows by it (`shadow accept --row ~<hash4>`, r1-seat-04.md). Without a stable ID, every reference is prose and ordinals renumber. The indictments hit the *recipe*, not the ID: nobody runs sha256, collisions go undetected, cross-branch identity inverts (r1-seat-06.md, Arg B); birthday math ~26% at 1,000 rows (r1-seat-09.md, Arg A) — but 1,000 *live* rows only happens if the mass cluster rejects archive-on-Close, and an ambiguous dereference greps as 2 hits: detectable, not silent. FOLD the mint recipe to one sentence: **"IDs are four base36 chars, unique in the plan, checked by lint; on collision, re-mint."** Delete the sha256 formula (r1-seat-06.md proposal, adopted verbatim).

**2. `needs:` — KEEP-CORE.**
Rent paid: it is the sole definition of "ready," which steering's entire grep depends on, and it is the mechanism that makes P0-before-polish executable law during the launch window — `C4 needs ~ag4k, ~pp2v` plus Close-outranks-Spike is CLAUDE.md §Scope Discipline as grammar, not vibes (r1-seat-05.md, Arg B). The chair misfiled this as concurrency machinery; a single seat deciding what to do next needs it just as much. The typo-dereference failure (r1-seat-06.md, Arg B) is already covered by the mechanical lint check "every `needs:` target resolves" — keep that check, in code not prose.

**3. `from:` — FOLD.**
Rent failed: it gates nothing by its own definition ("never blocks"), no seat cites one instance where it fed a decision, and the only mechanical reader in the product is grammar-blind to it (r1-seat-06.md, Arg C; r1-seat-04.md). Lineage is an *event* — events live in Progress. Exact fold sentence, into the discovered-work rule: **"Discovered work becomes a new row in the same cycle; its paired Progress line names the row it came from."** Token deleted from the row grammar.

**4. `size:` — DELETE (its one law FOLDS into the checkpoint definition).**
Rent failed: no seat evidences S-vs-M ever driving a decision; `L` is self-confessedly not data but a smell ("should be a milestone"); yet PLAN-LINT C mints findings about a field nothing consumes — manufacturing exactly the ignorable-lint drift seat-06 convicts (r1-seat-06.md, Arg C). Seat-02 used "size S = one sitting" only to compute steer frequency, an argument that survives without the token. Fold sentence, into Checkpoint: **"A checkpoint fits in one cycle; anything larger is a milestone."** Estimation theater from agents is noise; ties break toward fewer.

**5. `CLAIM` lines — DELETE (not defer).**
Rent failed three ways: it does not detect the collision it exists for (identical-edit silent merge, r1-seat-09.md Arg A); it is unpracticed even by its author (count=0 flagship, r1-seat-09.md Arg B) — which makes lint F's "PROOF without CLAIM" a permanent, ignored finding that trains every seat that lint is ignorable (r1-seat-06.md, Position); and no code touches it (r1-seat-04.md, Arg A). Deferring it to an appendix preserves a false promise — "when collisions come, use CLAIM" — when the stress seat proved CLAIM can't see the collision even then, and the defer trigger can never observably fire. Replacement core sentence: **"One seat writes a plan at a time; a second writer is a Contradictions row, not a merge."** If same-plan concurrency is ever truly needed, the reactivation is *mechanical*, not prose: seat-04's `shadow accept --row ~<hash4>` (proof rerun in clean checkout, receipt + flip in one commit) — code that refuses, where verbs can be rationalized around (r1-seat-04.md, Position). Parallelism stays below the identity line in disjoint plans and worktrees, which AGENT.md already mandates.

**6. `PROOF` lines — KEEP-CORE.**
The sole survivor of the verb trio, and the highest-rent token in this cluster: today's shipping lie (`C3~w5d9` Verified while AGENT.md is absent from the installed package) was exposed precisely by the proof-vs-claim distinction — the proof asserted plumbing, not world-state (r1-seat-09.md, Position; r1-seat-07.md, Arg A); the 70.4%-unverifiable ledger is the counterfactual world without proof receipts (r1-seat-08.md, Arg B); Close's audit chain "every DoD line traces to a PROOF line" and archive integrity both depend on it (r1-seat-03.md, Arg 3); and it is *practiced* grammar — the flagship writes PROOF lines even while skipping CLAIM. The proof-class extensions other clusters will rule on (`art:`+SEEN, r1-seat-01.md; `cmd/read/gate`, r1-seat-08.md) are evidence the slot is load-bearing, not evidence it is bloat.

**7. `DONE` lines — FOLD into the flip commit.**
Rent failed as a separate verb: under the single-writer law, DONE carries no information beyond flip+PROOF — the flip *is* done; flagship practice already degrades it to batched ceremony (one line, four rows, r1-seat-09.md Arg B), so even the author treats it as bookkeeping; its one legal function transfers verbatim onto PROOF. Exact fold sentence: **"A row flips to completed only in the same commit as its PROOF Progress line."** This also hands seat-04's proposal its enforcement point for free.

**8. one-claim-per-loop — FOLD into the checkpoint core, reworded without "claim."**
Rent paid: this is finish-before-start — Leo's named #1 pain (goal-looping vs finishing) — and the flagship's 4-row batch line proves the behavior needs a stated home (r1-seat-09.md, Arg B). But it is one behavioral sentence, not a standalone concept. Exact fold sentence, into the chair's concept 2: **"A cycle drives one checkpoint to a recorded result before starting the next."** Seat-02's serial-bottleneck indictment (3–6 folds/day ceiling, r1-seat-02.md Arg A) attacks fold-*ownership*, not this sentence; weakening it would reinstate the receipt-scatter that produced forty near-identical paragraphs in one day (r1-seat-02.md, Position).

---

**Net for this cluster:** 8 tokens → 3 core (hash-address, `needs:`, PROOF-with-flip), 4 folded to single sentences, CLAIM and the sha256 recipe and `size:` deleted. Nothing from this cluster goes to a concurrency appendix, because an appendix describing verbs that cannot detect their own failure mode is a false promise; the appendix slot, if the tribunal keeps one, should contain a single line: "same-plan concurrency is unsupported until a mechanical accept command exists."

**Dissent risk (3 sentences):** If the portfolio genuinely goes multi-seat on one plan before a mechanical `shadow accept` ships, deleting CLAIM removes the only — however leaky — intent signal, and double-work returns with no marker at all rather than a decaying one. If the mass cluster rejects archive-on-Close, live plans do reach 1,000+ rows and my hash-keep inherits the ~26% birthday odds I dismissed as archive-bounded. And folding DONE into the PROOF-flip commit assumes single-writer; if concurrency is later revived mechanically, that one-commit rule becomes the contention hotspot and would need to move inside the accept command's transaction.
