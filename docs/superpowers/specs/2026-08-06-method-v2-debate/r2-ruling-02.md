RULING — Cluster: modes & postures (Round 2, Shadow's Method simplification tribunal)

Concepts ruled: (1) the four-value `Mode:` vocabulary, (2) Spike, (3) Defer, (4) Challenge, (5) the transition law, (6) Close/"Jordan", (7) the chair's BROAD/CLOSING collapse.

---

**1. The four-mode `Mode:` vocabulary (Spike | Defer | Challenge | Close as legal plan states) — DELETE.**
Rent failed on three independent grounds. First, it is unenforced fiction in the field: r1-seat-06.md found the flagship's own PLAN.md speaks a pre-Method dialect with zero modes, and r1-seat-09.md found the actual plan corpus is prose vidux trees — no plan on this machine has ever held a Spike or Defer mode value, so two of the four values have literally never been load-bearing. Second, the scalar is race-blind: r1-seat-10.md proved a Close-vs-Challenge demotion and a close touch disjoint lines, merge cleanly, and lint F "fires only if A re-lints on a fresh read, which nothing forces" — the four-value transition table cannot be enforced by the machinery that exists. Third, the only place mode value feeds a decision — steering's "Close outranks Spike" — actively misroutes: r1-seat-02.md showed it legally steers the silent default into a StrongYes HOLD entity over a Snowcubes priority-1 row. A four-value vocabulary that is unwritten in the wild, unracing-safe, and wrong in its one ranking use pays no rent. The replacement is concept 7.

**2. Spike — FOLD into BROAD.**
Exact sentence: *"BROAD exploration is boxed up front and must end with a written keep / kill / promote verdict in Progress; a box past its end with no verdict is a lint finding."*
What pays rent is the box and the forced exit verdict — that is what the adversarial gate's "just exploring" answer lands on (chair core #5), and it survives fully as a behavior sentence. The mode wrapper pays nothing: no seat found a Spike-mode plan (r1-seat-06.md, r1-seat-09.md), and Spike's must-commit (pushed `spike/*` branch + log line) was always a write obligation, never a plan state. The chair is right that Spike is a broad-time behavior.

**3. Defer — FOLD into the Deferred-row write (chair core #4).**
Exact sentence: *"Defer is a write, never a state: one row — what | why-not-now | `wake: <predicate>` — and a row without a wake predicate is deletion in denial."*
Defer-as-mode was redundant with the Method's own grammar on day one: method.md already carries `## Deferred` as a section with the wake-predicate invariant, so the mode duplicated its own row type — exactly the internal-overlap disease r1-seat-05.md prosecuted in Drive. The fleet already treats Defer as a row property, not a plan state: r1-seat-07.md's constitution clause classifies rows as "Defer by definition." A whole-plan-deferred state is derivable at read time from its rows, consistent with "milestone status is derived, never stored." Mode-Defer deletes; row-Defer is core and untouched.

**4. Challenge — FOLD into BROAD plus the written-demotion law.**
Two exact sentences: *"Default posture is BROAD; its exit obligation is a verdict — questions asked, contradictions named, the decision each resolved into (a regenerated plan is a legal verdict)."* And: *"CLOSING is left only in writing: a surfaced contradiction demotes the plan to BROAD via a Progress line naming the contradiction, never silently."*
Challenge's rent is real — the interrogation record and being the demotion target — and both survive verbatim with BROAD substituted for CHALLENGE. The mode wrapper fails: r1-seat-10.md proved the guard "Close entered with open Challenge rows" cannot fire mechanically, and r1-seat-06.md showed `### Close` already collides with the `###` milestone namespace the transition record writes into. "Default when unclear: CHALLENGE" becomes "default posture is BROAD" — identical behavior, one fewer noun. This also answers r1-seat-06.md's cross-exam directly, and the answer belongs in the folded sentence: a proof string discovered rotted during CLOSING is a surfaced contradiction — the plan demotes to BROAD in writing, the proof is repaired there, and CLOSING is re-entered; the closer never rewrites the exam mid-sitting.

**5. The transition law (the 4×4 table + "transitions are the law, not the list") — FOLD into one sentence pair on the posture line.**
Exact sentences: *"The posture changes only via a paired Progress line landing in the same commit as the flip, and the flipping seat re-reads the posture line immediately before that commit (the own-row guard applies to the posture line)."* Plus the CLOSING entry half: *"CLOSING is entered only with a named harness."*
With two postures there are two edges, not twelve; everything enforceable in the old table was always just these clauses. r1-seat-10.md is the controlling evidence: the race survives ANY vocabulary size, and the same-commit pairing plus posture re-read-before-close is the only mechanical mitigation available without new tooling — it reuses the own-row guard the grammar already has, adding zero concepts. r1-seat-05.md's finding that the transition law was invisible to Drive dissolves with Drive's packet grammar (that cluster's ruling), leaving nothing for a standalone transition table to govern.

**6. Close / "Jordan" — KEEP-CORE, as the CLOSING posture, keeping the word "Close" on every rendered surface.**
This is the one concept in my cluster that earns its keep everywhere it is touched. It targets Leo's named top failure — agents confusing looping with finishing. It is the only posture with a mechanically checkable entry precondition (named harness) and exit artifact (coverage matrix, chair core #6). Every serious R1 remedy routes THROUGH Close rather than around it: r1-seat-03.md makes Close the mandatory compaction boundary (archive-on-Close), r1-seat-01.md hangs artifact-proof adjudication on the Close matrix, r1-seat-04.md shows Mode: Close + ranking IS the launch lockdown as executable law, and r1-seat-08.md's generality prosecution — hostile to nearly everything else — calls Close "the crown jewel" that holds even for non-code work. Today's live shipping lie (C3~w5d9/DoD d1 Verified while the installed package ships no AGENT.md — r1-seat-07.md, r1-seat-10.md) is not evidence against Close; it is evidence that Close's proof-line requirement is the only gate that could ever have caught it, and that its proof strings must assert world-state, not plumbing (that repair belongs to the proof cluster). Ruling on r1-seat-04.md's cross-exam, which is mine to answer: when every agent-side row is Verified and the DoD sits LEO-GATED with a named handoff and resume predicate, the plan CLOSES on the agent side and goal-chaining mints the successor — hanging in CLOSING awaiting a human click is precisely the unfinishable loop Close exists to kill, and the chair's core-6 wording already licenses this.

**7. The chair's BROAD/CLOSING two-posture collapse + Defer-as-write — KEEP-CORE (adopt).**
It is Leo's own fragment ("broad vs closing time") returned to him; it preserves every enforceable clause of the old law as folded sentences (rulings 2–5); it shrinks the raced scalar's vocabulary and the steering rank sentence to "CLOSING outranks BROAD" — which, per r1-seat-02.md, must rank BELOW entity priority, but that is the steering cluster's row. One consequential edit this ruling orders: the board chip and all prose keep the word "Close" for the CLOSING posture — r1-seat-08.md showed the mode chips are already agent jargon to the second-most-important user; renaming Leo's one beloved noun to a gerund would be churn with negative rent.
