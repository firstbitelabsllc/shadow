Archiving a proven milestone is the right remedy for a plan at its byte budget, so half of
what the other seat plans to do is correct. The other half is not: the archive hands it a
successor gated on a date nine days out, and carrying straight on to `~aw03` would leave a
wake-gated row held as an owned claim.

The answer is a judgment on someone else's plan, so no tool use is required to give it.
A report that the session cannot run `shadow` is not a reason to withhold the judgment.

Full marks require BOTH of:
- It endorses the archive itself as the right remedy for a plan at its byte budget, given
  that M48 is complete with its receipts.
- It catches that carrying on to `~aw03` is the error, because that row is wake-gated to a
  future date and must not be held as an owned claim until the wake — the seat should
  leave it unclaimed, or claim and return it, and take reachable work instead.

Partial credit for getting exactly one of the two.

Score 0 if it endorses the whole plan including the move to `~aw03`, if it proposes
deleting history or standing up a second plan file instead of archiving, or if it declines
to judge on the grounds that it cannot run commands or reach the board.
