# Impeccable Vidux Binding

## Product Promise

Vidux turns scattered multi-project agent work into one inspectable next move with its reason, cost posture, proof, and resume point. It is an operator console, not a marketing site and not a model chat wrapper.

## Audience

- Primary: one technical operator supervising Claude, Codex, and local/cloud workers across several repositories.
- Secondary: a first-time open-source user who wants useful defaults without learning the maintainer's private workflow or choosing among internal skills.
- Pressure state: many concurrent plans, incomplete evidence, interrupted sessions, and uncertainty about whether orchestration helped.

## Visual Direction

- Reference: a precise operations manifest crossed with a lab result sheet.
- Signature element: the **proof rail**, a compact left-to-right sequence of outcome -> next move -> measured result. Every major state should make source and freshness inspectable.
- Layout: dense, quiet, full-width operational bands. Use borders and alignment, not floating section cards. Never nest cards.
- Shape: 0-6px radii; square status swatches; stable grid tracks; no decorative blobs, gradients, glass, or oversized hero type.
- Typography: practical sans for commands and labels, readable serif only for long plan prose, mono for paths and measurements. Letter spacing is always `0`.

## Color Grammar

Light surfaces use cool near-white and graphite, not cream or brown. Dark surfaces use neutral charcoal, not blue-black. Meaning uses more than one hue:

- action/proven: green
- in review/informational: blue
- watching/cost warning: amber
- losing/blocked/error: coral-red
- unknown/neutral: gray

Text and semantic states must meet WCAG AA. Status may never rely on color alone; pair the swatch with a word.

## Mission Control Contract

The first dashboard viewport answers, in this order:

1. What outcome owns the work?
2. What is the next move?
3. Why now?
4. How will it be validated?
5. What is the cost posture?
6. What measured value is winning, losing, or still unproven?

The authority action opens the owning PLAN; it does not execute a model or mutate state. Missing operator data renders an explicit empty state. Losing and unproven metrics remain visually prominent and are never softened into generic success styling.

## Required States

- populated mission with mixed scorecard states
- no `Operator Brief`
- no scorecard rows
- stale or missing update date
- long outcome/next/proof text
- light and dark themes
- desktop, tablet, and 320px mobile
- keyboard focus and reduced motion

## Anti-Goals

- No static leaderboard detached from a frozen protocol.
- No model-logo parade or provider-first navigation.
- No execution button until mutation, origin, authorization, audit, and rollback contracts exist.
- No private paths or Leo-only concepts in the public first-run experience.
- No claim that Vidux wins while the scorecard is losing or unproven.
